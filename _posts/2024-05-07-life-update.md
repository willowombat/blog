so I have not posted in a long time. I have been through a lot lately. My husband of 13 years, Joseph Watson died last December of complications with cancer. It completely flipped me over psychologically. I couldn't bear to stay in our apartment any longer so I broke my lease 5 months early and left thousands of dollars of furniture and electronics etc, taking only a few mementos of Joe and our 2 dogs and relocating to Las Vegas. I have reliable friends and family here who are helping me with my recovery, and I am grateful for that. I tried taking a job at an old company I had worked at before, perhaps trying to find a happier period in my life. It didn't work out, I was supposed to be a Lab Manager assistant instead of a regular Lab Manager as I was used to, but the new Lab Manager was extremely religious, and while I don't want to specifically say he was prejudiced against me because I'm gay, he did have a "turn to Jesus" sign posted up in the lab and several people who worked in the lab were members of his congregation. Ultimately it didn't work out because the only person he would accept as an assistant would be an exact clone of himself. I was promised by the company that I would get assistance and training in my new role and have a 90-day review, but the religious lab manager barely gave me 6 working days before he held my review saying it wasn't working out. I thought about trying to defend myself, but I was still just too heartbroken over Joe passing away and the fact that I knew right away the management was going to let this guy have his way, instead of honoring the terms of my offer letter. I transferred to a different company shortly after, again as Lab Manager, which was a little bit of a relief.

Joe and I were a happy couple, we fit together like a hand and glove, and as we were getting to know each other, "I" became "we" very easily. Now I have to get used to being just an "I" again, and I doubt I will ever find love again like how I had it with Joe. Healing from something like this is hard, and requires a lot of intentional and concentrated effort on becoming better, and focusing on happier things. I don't know how exactly I am going to continue on without him, some days I feel like just giving up and letting my neglected health problems take me early, but I am trying, and it is a day-to-day process. I am trying my best to put one front in front of the other, and then repeating that, to see what happens.
