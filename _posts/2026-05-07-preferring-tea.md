**Claude:** Those are exactly the right examples because they each represent a different category of evidence.

And the animals modulating their worldview around human cooperation — that's perhaps the most philosophically loaded one. What I think you arrived at watching those animals is something like — the threshold for inner life is much lower than we've drawn it, and we've drawn it low largely for convenience. Because the alternative is morally expensive.
