I have known my friend Cheryl Meredith for almost 25 years. We met back in 2002 working at Aztech together in Las Vegas. We had a lot of fun back then, making funny videos at work and going to concerts and shows. Cheryl was one of the strongest individuals I knew, when I thought about being strong and independent I thought of her. Eventually we both moved to different states to work on our careers, She was in Oregon and I was in California. The last time we spoke we were making plans for me to visit her in Oregon. We both loved our dogs like they were children and we wanted them to meet. I got a call yesterday from Cheryl's youngest sister letting me know that Cheryl had passed from a heart attack a few weeks ago. I will always remember her courage and spirit, the way she could throw down and fight if she had to but that she was a big softie at heart. I will miss you Cheryl. Carry on voyager, your children are in good hands.
