For the first time in a long time, I feel a change. My energy level is slowly rising off the floor, and I am starting to feel something akin to hopeful. My spouses cancer ordeal had been more draining on me than I anticipated, and think it brought on another cycle of depression. It's not his fault of course, and I would do anything to take care of him, but part me wants this to be over and I hope we can see the light at the end of the tunnel soon. I have started a new job, at a great new company, in a great new city and can't wait to start exploring. One of my advisors told me that I am in the transition phase to becoming an elder, and that I need to embrace that change. I can't wait to start it.
